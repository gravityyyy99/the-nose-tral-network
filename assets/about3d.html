Creator of The Nose-tral Network and its AI, Aroma-GPT.

MY VISION

Teach machines to smell

Smell is the sense machines understand least. I want to show it can be taught, and to explain it so clearly that anyone can follow along, not just chemists or engineers.

WHAT I MADE

Aroma-GPT

A smell model that reads molecules, an electronic nose with its own model that works from sensor readings alone, and the site and documentation book around them.

WHAT I WAS THINKING

Show it all, honestly

I wanted the whole chain from a word to a guess in one place. The nose is simulated for now, and I publish where the models fail as well as where they work.

WHAT COMES NEXT

A real smelling device

The next step is real hardware, tested properly, with every result shared here. This is a fully fledged project, not a one-off demo.
